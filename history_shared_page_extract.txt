📖 Course Objectives
To understand the historical background and significance of the Arab conquest of Sindh.
To analyze the political, social, and cultural impact of early Muslim rule in the subcontinent.
To trace the establishment, expansion, and consolidation of the Delhi Sultanate.
To critically evaluate the rise, grandeur, and decline of the Mughal Empire.
To explore the socio-religious and cultural transformations during the Mughal period.
