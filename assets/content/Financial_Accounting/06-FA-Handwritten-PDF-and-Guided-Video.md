# FA Handwritten Notes — PDF and Guided Video

## What is included

This lecture contains the original unique handwritten Financial Accounting pages collected from the supplied notes archive. The pages are ordered as **FA_Handwritten_Notes_Ordered.pdf** and are also displayed inside the app as an image gallery so you can zoom and review them offline.

## How to study this lecture

1. Play **FA Handwritten Notes — Guided Lecture** first. The video explains the page sequence in Roman Urdu plus English.
2. Pause the video whenever a journal entry, debit-credit table, ledger/T-account, trial balance, payable, receivable, or statement format appears.
3. Copy the example into your notebook and solve it without looking.
4. Reopen the handwritten gallery and compare your working with the original page.
5. Finish by revising the FA entries-and-statements practice lecture.

## Offline PDF asset

The ordered PDF is bundled at:

`assets/content/Financial_Accounting/FA_Handwritten_Notes_Ordered.pdf`

The app also exposes the same pages through the **Original handwritten pages** gallery. This is useful on Chrome and Android because the pages remain available offline after the asset bundle is installed.

## Passing focus

Do not only memorize the handwriting. Practice the full chain: transaction analysis, journal entry, ledger posting, trial balance, adjustments, Income Statement, and Balance Sheet. In the exam, write the rule first, show the debit and credit amounts clearly, and check that both sides are equal.
