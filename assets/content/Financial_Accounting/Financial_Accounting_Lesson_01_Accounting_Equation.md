# Financial Accounting — Lesson 01
## Accounting Equation: Sab se pehla foundation

### 1. Accounting kya hoti hai?

Accounting ka simple matlab hai business ke paison se related transactions ko identify, record, classify, summarize aur report karna. Roman Urdu mein: business mein paisa kahan se aya, kahan gaya, kitna bacha, aur business ke paas kya assets hain—ye sab accounting batati hai.

### 2. Teen basic words

**Asset:** Business ke paas maujood valuable cheez. Examples: cash, bank balance, furniture, equipment, building, aur receivables.

**Liability:** Business ka kisi doosre person ya organization ko dena wala paisa. Examples: loan, payable, aur udhaar.

**Capital/Equity:** Owner ka business mein lagaya hua haq ya investment. Simple formula: owner ki ownership.

### 3. Main formula

> **Assets = Liabilities + Capital**

Is formula ko accounting equation kehte hain. Business ki har transaction ke baad equation balance rehni chahiye.

### 4. Simple example

Agar owner ne business mein **Rs. 100,000 cash** invest kiya:

- Asset: Cash +100,000
- Capital: +100,000
- Equation: Assets 100,000 = Liabilities 0 + Capital 100,000

Agar business ne **Rs. 30,000 ka equipment cash par** khareeda:

- Cash -30,000
- Equipment +30,000
- Total assets same rehte hain: 100,000
- Equation ab bhi balance hai.

Agar business ne **Rs. 20,000 ka loan** liya:

- Cash +20,000
- Liability/Loan +20,000
- Assets 120,000 = Liabilities 20,000 + Capital 100,000

### 5. Revenue, expense aur drawings

**Revenue** business ki earning hoti hai. Revenue capital ko increase karta hai.

**Expense** business ka kharcha hota hai, jaise rent, salary, electricity aur transport. Expense capital ko reduce karta hai.

**Drawings** owner ka business se personal use ke liye paisa ya asset nikalna hai. Drawings bhi capital ko reduce karta hai.

Extended formula:

> **Assets = Liabilities + Capital + Revenue − Expenses − Drawings**

### 6. Exam answer format

Agar exam mein “Define accounting equation” aaye to likho:

“Accounting equation is the basic relationship between assets, liabilities, and owner's capital. It is expressed as Assets = Liabilities + Capital. Every business transaction keeps this equation balanced.”

Roman Urdu explanation: Accounting equation business ke assets ko liabilities aur owner ke capital ke saath relate karti hai. Har transaction ke baad dono sides equal rehni chahiye.

### 7. Practice questions

1. Accounting equation kya hoti hai?
2. Assets, liabilities aur capital ki definition likho.
3. Owner Rs. 50,000 cash invest karta hai. Equation par effect batao.
4. Business Rs. 10,000 ka furniture cash par khareedta hai. Kya total assets change honge?
5. Revenue aur expense capital ko kaise affect karte hain?

### 8. Quick revision

- Assets business ki valuable cheezen hain.
- Liabilities business ke debts hain.
- Capital owner ka investment/equity hai.
- Main formula: Assets = Liabilities + Capital.
- Revenue capital increase karta hai.
- Expenses aur drawings capital reduce karte hain.
- Har transaction ke baad equation balance honi chahiye.
