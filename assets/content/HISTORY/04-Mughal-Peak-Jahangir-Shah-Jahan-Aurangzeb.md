# Lecture 4 — Mughal Peak: Jahangir, Shah Jahan and Aurangzeb

## Jahangir, 1605–1627

Jahangir continued many administrative traditions of Akbar. Nur Jahan had strong influence in court politics and administration. For exams, discuss continuity as well as the role of court culture and painting.

## Shah Jahan, 1628–1658

Shah Jahan's period is commonly associated with imperial power, architecture, and the so-called Golden Age of Mughal architecture. Taj Mahal and Red Fort are important examples. Architecture should be linked with economy, royal patronage, craftsmanship, and political symbolism.

## Aurangzeb Alamgir, 1658–1707

Aurangzeb expanded Mughal control into the Deccan and followed a more explicitly Islamic political approach. His long campaigns increased pressure on the empire's finances and administration. A balanced answer should discuss both territorial expansion and the challenges it produced.

## Compare the three rulers

Jahangir is remembered for continuation and court culture; Shah Jahan for architecture and imperial grandeur; Aurangzeb for expansion and the beginning of serious pressures on the empire. Avoid writing only praise or criticism; compare policies, achievements, and long-term effects.
