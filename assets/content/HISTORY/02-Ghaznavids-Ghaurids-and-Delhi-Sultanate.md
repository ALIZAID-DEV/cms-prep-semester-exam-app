# Lecture 2 — Ghaznavids, Ghaurids and Delhi Sultanate

## Ghaznavid rule

Arab rule in Sindh gradually changed over time. Later Turkish power became important. Sultan Mahmud Ghaznavi ruled from 998 to 1030 and conducted campaigns in the subcontinent. Exam mein invasions ke causes, political impact, economic effects, aur cultural influence ko separate headings ke saath explain karein.

## Ghaurid expansion

Muhammad Ghori ki conquests ne North India mein permanent Muslim political rule ki foundation strong ki. Ghaurid period ke baad unke commanders ne Delhi Sultanate ko organize kiya.

## Delhi Sultanate, 1206–1526

Delhi Sultanate ke major dynasties Slave or Mamluk, Khalji, Tughluq, Sayyid, aur Lodhi thay. Qutb-ud-din Aibak ne early foundation rakhi, Iltutmish ne administration consolidate ki, aur Balban ne central authority ko strengthen kiya.

Alauddin Khalji expansion, market reforms aur military organization ke liye important hai. Muhammad bin Tughluq ki policies ambitious thin, lekin kuch experiments practical problems ki wajah se fail hue. Sayyid aur Lodhi periods mein Sultanate weak hui. Timur ki invasion aur internal conflicts ke baad 1526 mein Lodhi rule ka end hua.

## Administration and culture

Delhi Sultanate ne Persian administrative culture, new architectural forms, urban centres, aur political institutions introduce kiye. Qutb Minar ko architecture ke example ke taur par yaad rakhein.

## Exam questions

1. Delhi Sultanate ki major dynasties compare karein.
2. Alauddin Khalji ke reforms explain karein.
3. Muhammad bin Tughluq ki policies ka critical review likhein.
4. Delhi Sultanate ke decline ke causes likhein.
