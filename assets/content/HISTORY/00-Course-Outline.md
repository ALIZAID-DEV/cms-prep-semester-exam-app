# History — Course Outline

## Working exam scope

This outline is a **working preparation outline**, inferred from the shared History material supplied by the student. It should be checked against the official CMS College / University of Sindh course outline when available.

## Main periods

1. Advent of Islam in Sindh and early Muslim rule, beginning with the Arab conquest of Sindh in 712 CE.
2. Ghaznavid and Ghaurid expansion and the foundation of Muslim rule in North India.
3. Delhi Sultanate from 1206 to 1526, including the Slave, Khalji, Tughluq, Sayyid, and Lodhi dynasties.
4. Mughal foundation and consolidation from 1526 onward.
5. Mughal peak under Jahangir, Shah Jahan, and Aurangzeb.
6. Decline of the Mughal Empire, the growth of the East India Company, and the end of Mughal rule after 1857.
7. Comparative question: economic and religious transformation of the Aryans between Early and Later Vedic India.

## Exam method

For every long question, write a short introduction, use chronological headings, explain causes and effects, and finish with historical significance. Memorize dates only with the related event. Use Roman Urdu explanations for revision, but write final exam answers in the language required by the paper.
