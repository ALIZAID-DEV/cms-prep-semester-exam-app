                          Lecture 11: Computer Security
🔹 Computer Security
    Protection against damage or theft of hardware, software, and information.
    Prevents threats from viruses, malware, and unauthorized use.

🔹 Cybercrime
    Crimes committed using computers and networks.
    Examples: hacking, phishing, online fraud.

🔹 Hackers vs Crackers
   Hacker: Skilled person who identifies and exploits network loopholes.
   Cracker: Gains unauthorized access to systems with malicious intent.

🔹 Phishing
    Fake emails or websites used to steal personal information.

🔹 Cyberbullying & Harassment
    Using electronic means (computers, mobiles, internet) to threaten or harass.
    Victims should report to trusted people or authorities.

🔹 Cyber-Attack
    Criminals use devices to attack single or multiple networks.

🔹 Malware
   Malicious software: viruses, worms, spyware, adware.
   Spread via USBs, CDs, downloads, networks, email attachments.

🔹 Antivirus Software
    Utility software that protects computers from malware and viruses.

🔹 Data Safety
    Important files should be backed up in multiple locations.

🔹 Authentication Mechanisms
      Hardware/software systems that ensure only authorized users access data.

🔹 Professional Ethics
    Principles and rules guiding professional behavior in computing.

🔹 Information Accuracy
    Ensures information is correct and reliable.

🔹 Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
    Protects intangible creations (software, designs, writings).
    Enforced through copyrights, patents, trademarks.

🔹 Software Piracy
    Illegal use, copying, or distribution of copyrighted software.

🔹 Plagiarism
    Presenting someone else’s work or ideas without proper acknowledgment.
