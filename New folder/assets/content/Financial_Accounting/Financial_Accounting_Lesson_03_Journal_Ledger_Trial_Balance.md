# Financial Accounting — Lesson 03
## Journal, Ledger, Trial Balance aur Accounting Cycle

### 1. Accounting cycle kya hota hai?

Accounting cycle business transaction se start hota hai aur financial statements par end hota hai. Simple chain yaad rakho:

> Transaction → Journal → Ledger → Trial Balance → Adjustments → Financial Statements → Closing Entries

### 2. General Journal

General journal ko book of original entry bhi kehte hain. Har transaction sab se pehle journal mein date order ke mutabiq record hoti hai.

Journal ke basic columns hotay hain: Date, Account Title, Debit, Credit aur Narration.

Example: Owner ne Rs. 50,000 cash invest kiya.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 50,000 | — |
| Capital | — | 50,000 |

Narration: Being cash invested by owner.

### 3. Ledger aur T-account

Ledger mein har individual account ka separate record hota hai. Journal se entries ledger accounts mein post hoti hain. Ledger ka purpose har account ka balance find karna hai.

T-account ke left side ko debit side aur right side ko credit side kehte hain.

Example: Agar Cash account mein Rs. 50,000 debit aur Rs. 4,000 credit ho, to cash balance Rs. 46,000 debit hoga.

### 4. Trial Balance

Trial balance ledger balances ki list hoti hai. Is ka main purpose check karna hai ke total debits total credits ke equal hain ya nahi.

> **Total Debit Balances = Total Credit Balances**

Trial balance equal ho to arithmetic posting likely correct hoti hai, lekin har error detect nahi hota. Misal ke taur par transaction completely miss ho jaye to trial balance phir bhi equal ho sakta hai.

### 5. Adjusting entries

Accounting period ke end par kuch revenues aur expenses update karne ke liye adjusting entries banayi jati hain. Examples: accrued expense, prepaid expense, depreciation aur accrued revenue.

Adjusting entries ka purpose ye hai ke revenue aur expense usi period mein record hon jis period se woh related hain.

### 6. Adjusted trial balance

Adjustments post karne ke baad adjusted trial balance prepare hota hai. Isi updated trial balance se financial statements prepare ki jati hain.

### 7. Financial statements

**Income Statement:** Revenue aur expenses ko compare karke net income ya net loss show karta hai.

**Statement of Retained Earnings:** Retained earnings mein changes show karta hai.

**Balance Sheet:** Assets, liabilities aur owner's equity ki financial position show karti hai.

### 8. Closing entries

Closing entries temporary accounts, jaise revenue, expenses aur drawings, ko close karti hain. New accounting period mein temporary accounts ka balance zero se start hota hai. Permanent accounts, jaise assets aur liabilities, continue rehte hain.

### 9. Solved mini-cycle

Transactions:

1. Owner invested Rs. 100,000 cash.
2. Equipment bought for Rs. 30,000 cash.
3. Rent paid Rs. 5,000 cash.
4. Services provided for Rs. 20,000 cash.

Journal entries:

| Transaction | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Owner investment | Cash 100,000 | Capital 100,000 |
| Equipment purchase | Equipment 30,000 | Cash 30,000 |
| Rent payment | Rent Expense 5,000 | Cash 5,000 |
| Service revenue | Cash 20,000 | Service Revenue 20,000 |

Ending cash = 100,000 − 30,000 − 5,000 + 20,000 = **Rs. 85,000**.

Net income = Revenue 20,000 − Expense 5,000 = **Rs. 15,000**.
