# CMS Prep — Financial Accounting Complete Exam Roadmap

## Goal

Is roadmap ka goal Financial Accounting ko bilkul beginner level se final-exam level tak samajhna hai. Har topic Roman Urdu + English explanation, solved examples, flashcards aur revision ke saath cover hoga. Subject-wise MCQs alag practice section mein available hain.

## Core lecture sequence

### Video 1 — Accounting Foundation
Accounting ka meaning, users, organization types, assets, liabilities, capital, revenue, expenses, accounting equation, aur basic transaction analysis.

### Video 2 — Double Entry and Debit/Credit
Double-entry system, account types, debit/credit rules, Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, revenue, expense, drawings, aur basic journal entries.

### Video 3 — Complete Accounting Cycle
General journal, ledger posting, T-accounts, balances, trial balance, adjusting entries, adjusted trial balance, financial statements aur closing entries.

## Must-practice transaction patterns

1. Owner cash investment.
2. Cash purchase of equipment.
3. Credit purchase and Accounts Payable.
4. Credit sale and Accounts Receivable.
5. Cash sale and revenue.
6. Rent, salary, electricity and other expenses.
7. Owner drawings.
8. Loan received and loan repayment.
9. Customer payment received.
10. Supplier payment made.

## Final exam checklist

Student ko exam se pehle accounting equation, debit/credit rules, journal entries, ledger, trial balance, adjustments, income statement, balance sheet aur closing entries independently solve karne aane chahiye.
