## Solved transaction practice

### Exercise A

Transactions:

1. Owner invested Rs. 100,000 cash.
2. Bought furniture for Rs. 20,000 cash.
3. Purchased supplies on credit for Rs. 8,000.
4. Provided services for Rs. 25,000 cash.
5. Paid rent of Rs. 5,000.
6. Paid supplier Rs. 3,000.
7. Owner withdrew Rs. 4,000 for personal use.

Journal solution:

| No. | Debit | Credit |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Cash Dr. 100,000 | Capital Cr. 100,000 |
| 2 | Furniture Dr. 20,000 | Cash Cr. 20,000 |
| 3 | Supplies Dr. 8,000 | Accounts Payable Cr. 8,000 |
| 4 | Cash Dr. 25,000 | Service Revenue Cr. 25,000 |
| 5 | Rent Expense Dr. 5,000 | Cash Cr. 5,000 |
| 6 | Accounts Payable Dr. 3,000 | Cash Cr. 3,000 |
| 7 | Drawings Dr. 4,000 | Cash Cr. 4,000 |

Ending cash = 100,000 − 20,000 + 25,000 − 5,000 − 3,000 − 4,000 = **Rs. 93,000**.
