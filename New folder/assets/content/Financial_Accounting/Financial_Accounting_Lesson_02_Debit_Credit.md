# Financial Accounting — Lesson 02
## Double Entry, Debit/Credit, Accounts Payable aur Accounts Receivable

### 1. Double-entry system kya hai?

Double-entry system ka matlab hai ke har business transaction ke kam az kam do effects hote hain. Ek effect ko **Debit (Dr.)** aur doosre effect ko **Credit (Cr.)** record kiya jata hai. Is se accounting equation balance rehti hai.

> **Total Debits = Total Credits**

Debit ka matlab hamesha “acha” aur credit ka matlab hamesha “bura” nahi hota. Debit aur credit sirf accounting record ki do sides ke naam hain.

### 2. Basic debit and credit rules

| Account type | Increase | Decrease |
|---|---|---|
| Asset | Debit | Credit |
| Expense | Debit | Credit |
| Liability | Credit | Debit |
| Capital/Equity | Credit | Debit |
| Revenue/Income | Credit | Debit |

Yaad rakhne ka simple rule: **Assets aur Expenses increase hon to Debit. Liabilities, Capital aur Revenue increase hon to Credit.**

### 3. Accounts Receivable

**Accounts Receivable** ka matlab hai customers se business ko paisa receive karna hai. Ye business ka **asset** hota hai, kyunki future mein business ko cash milne ki expectation hoti hai.

Example: Business ne customer ko Rs. 5,000 ka maal credit par sell kiya.

- Accounts Receivable Dr. Rs. 5,000
- Sales Revenue Cr. Rs. 5,000

Jab customer baad mein cash pay kare:

- Cash Dr. Rs. 5,000
- Accounts Receivable Cr. Rs. 5,000

### 4. Accounts Payable

**Accounts Payable** ka matlab hai business ko supplier ko paisa pay karna hai. Ye business ki **liability** hoti hai, kyunki business future mein payment karega.

Example: Business ne supplier se Rs. 8,000 ka equipment credit par khareeda.

- Equipment Dr. Rs. 8,000
- Accounts Payable Cr. Rs. 8,000

Jab business supplier ko payment kare:

- Accounts Payable Dr. Rs. 8,000
- Cash Cr. Rs. 8,000

### 5. Journal entry ka simple method

Har transaction ke liye ye four steps follow karo:

1. Transaction mein affected accounts identify karo.
2. Har account ka type identify karo: asset, liability, capital, revenue ya expense.
3. Dekho account increase hua ya decrease.
4. Debit/Credit table se correct side choose karo.

### 6. Solved examples

**Example 1: Owner invested Rs. 50,000 cash.**

- Cash asset increase hua: Cash Dr. 50,000
- Capital increase hua: Capital Cr. 50,000

**Example 2: Rent paid Rs. 4,000 cash.**

- Rent expense increase hua: Rent Expense Dr. 4,000
- Cash asset decrease hua: Cash Cr. 4,000

**Example 3: Office furniture bought for Rs. 10,000 cash.**

- Furniture asset increase hua: Furniture Dr. 10,000
- Cash asset decrease hua: Cash Cr. 10,000

### 7. Exam answer format

**Question: Define double-entry system.**

“Double-entry system is an accounting system in which every business transaction affects at least two accounts. One account is debited and another account is credited. The total debit always remains equal to the total credit.”

Roman Urdu: Double-entry ka matlab hai har transaction ko do sides par record karna, taake accounts balance rahen.
