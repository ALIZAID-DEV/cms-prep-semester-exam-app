# FA Lecture 4 — Entries, Ledger, Trial Balance and Statements

## 1. The accounting cycle in simple words

Har transaction ko pehle **analyze** karo, phir journal mein record karo, journal se ledger mein post karo, ledger balances se trial balance banao, adjustments record karo, aur end par Income Statement aur Balance Sheet prepare karo.

> Transaction analysis → Journal entry → Ledger posting → Trial balance → Adjustments → Financial statements → Closing entries

## 2. Double-entry ka golden rule

Har transaction ke kam az kam do effects hote hain. Total debit hamesha total credit ke equal hota hai. Debit ka matlab automatically loss nahi hota aur credit ka matlab automatically profit nahi hota; debit aur credit account type aur transaction ke effect par depend karte hain.

| Account type | Increase | Decrease | Normal balance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asset | Debit | Credit | Debit |
| Expense | Debit | Credit | Debit |
| Drawings | Debit | Credit | Debit |
| Liability | Credit | Debit | Credit |
| Capital/Equity | Credit | Debit | Credit |
| Revenue | Credit | Debit | Credit |

## 3. Transaction analysis method

Har question mein yeh four steps likho:

1. Kaun se accounts change hue?
2. Har account asset, liability, capital, revenue, expense ya drawings hai?
3. Account increase hua ya decrease?
4. Debit-credit rule apply karo aur equal amounts check karo.

## 4. Solved journal entries

Assume business ka naam **Mickey Traders** hai.

| Date | Transaction | Debit entry | Credit entry | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Owner invested cash Rs. 100,000 | Cash Dr. 100,000 | Capital Cr. 100,000 | Asset aur owner equity increase |
| 2 | Bought furniture for cash Rs. 20,000 | Furniture Dr. 20,000 | Cash Cr. 20,000 | Furniture increase, cash decrease |
| 3 | Bought goods on credit from Ali Rs. 15,000 | Purchases Dr. 15,000 | Accounts Payable—Ali Cr. 15,000 | Goods/purchases increase, supplier liability increase |
| 4 | Sold goods for cash Rs. 24,000 | Cash Dr. 24,000 | Sales Cr. 24,000 | Cash aur revenue increase |
| 5 | Sold services on credit to Sara Rs. 12,000 | Accounts Receivable—Sara Dr. 12,000 | Service Revenue Cr. 12,000 | Customer se amount receive hona baqi hai |
| 6 | Paid rent Rs. 5,000 | Rent Expense Dr. 5,000 | Cash Cr. 5,000 | Expense increase, cash decrease |
| 7 | Received Rs. 7,000 from Sara | Cash Dr. 7,000 | Accounts Receivable—Sara Cr. 7,000 | Customer balance reduce |
| 8 | Paid Ali Rs. 9,000 | Accounts Payable—Ali Dr. 9,000 | Cash Cr. 9,000 | Supplier liability reduce |
| 9 | Owner withdrew cash Rs. 4,000 | Drawings Dr. 4,000 | Cash Cr. 4,000 | Owner's personal withdrawal |
| 10 | Bought supplies on credit Rs. 3,000 | Supplies Dr. 3,000 | Accounts Payable Cr. 3,000 | Supplies increase, payable increase |

### Important difference

**Accounts Receivable** ka matlab customer se paisa lena hai, is liye yeh asset hai aur normally debit balance rakhta hai. **Accounts Payable** ka matlab supplier ko paisa dena hai, is liye yeh liability hai aur normally credit balance rakhta hai.

## 5. Ledger posting

Journal entry ke baad har account ka separate ledger/T-account banao. T-account ke left side ko debit aur right side ko credit kehte hain.

### Cash Account

| Debit side | Rs. | Credit side | Rs. |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Capital | 100,000 | Furniture | 20,000 |
| Sales | 24,000 | Rent Expense | 5,000 |
| Accounts Receivable—Sara | 7,000 | Accounts Payable—Ali | 9,000 |
| | | Drawings | 4,000 |
| **Total** | **131,000** | **Total** | **38,000** |
| | | **Balance c/d** | **93,000** |

Cash balance = Rs. 93,000 debit. Ledger balance nikalte waqt jis side ka total zyada ho, difference us side ke opposite balance c/d ke naam se show hota hai.

### Accounts Receivable—Sara

| Debit | Rs. | Credit | Rs. |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Service Revenue | 12,000 | Cash received | 7,000 |
| | | **Balance c/d** | **5,000** |

Sara se ab Rs. 5,000 receive karna baqi hai.

### Accounts Payable—Ali

| Debit | Rs. | Credit | Rs. |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Cash paid | 9,000 | Purchases | 15,000 |
| **Balance c/d** | **6,000** | | |

Ali ko ab Rs. 6,000 pay karna baqi hai.

## 6. Trial balance

Trial balance ledger ke closing balances ki list hoti hai. Iska basic purpose arithmetic accuracy check karna hai: total debit total credit ke equal hona chahiye. Equal totals ka matlab yeh nahi ke har error impossible hai; transaction completely miss ho jaye to trial balance phir bhi balance kar sakta hai.

## 7. Adjusting entries

Adjusting entries period ke end par is liye banai jati hain ke income aur expenses correct period mein show hon.

| Adjustment | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Outstanding salary Rs. 2,000 | Salary Expense Dr. 2,000 | Salary Payable Cr. 2,000 |
| Prepaid insurance used Rs. 1,000 | Insurance Expense Dr. 1,000 | Prepaid Insurance Cr. 1,000 |
| Depreciation Rs. 3,000 | Depreciation Expense Dr. 3,000 | Accumulated Depreciation Cr. 3,000 |
| Unearned revenue earned Rs. 4,000 | Unearned Revenue Dr. 4,000 | Service Revenue Cr. 4,000 |

## 8. Income Statement and Balance Sheet connection

Income Statement mein revenues minus expenses se net income ya net loss nikalta hai. Balance Sheet mein assets, liabilities aur owner's equity show hoti hai.

**Income Statement formula:**

> Net income = Total revenue − Total expenses

**Balance Sheet formula:**

> Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity

Closing process mein temporary accounts—revenue, expenses aur drawings—close kiye jate hain. Assets, liabilities aur capital permanent accounts hote hain aur next period mein carry forward hote hain.

## Last-minute memory line

**Assets and expenses increase on debit. Liabilities, capital and revenue increase on credit. Journal records, ledger classifies, trial balance checks, and statements report.**
