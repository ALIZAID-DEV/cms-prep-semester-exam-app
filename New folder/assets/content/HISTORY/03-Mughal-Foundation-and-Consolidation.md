# Lecture 3 — Foundation and Consolidation of the Mughal Empire

## Babur, 1526–1530

Mughal rule in India began after Babur's victory in the First Battle of Panipat in 1526. Babur's military strategy, field artillery, and leadership helped defeat Ibrahim Lodhi. In an exam answer, explain both the battle and its historical result: the establishment of a new empire.

## Humayun, 1530–1556

Humayun faced political pressure and temporary displacement. The Suri interlude interrupted Mughal rule, but Humayun later returned and restored the dynasty.

## Akbar the Great, 1556–1605

Akbar expanded the empire and developed administrative systems. The **Mansabdari system** organized ranks and military service. His religious policy and interest in cultural cooperation were also significant. Din-i-Ilahi is often discussed as an example of his religious experiment, but answers should explain its limited scope and historical context.

## Exam approach

A strong answer connects conquest, administration, and cultural development. Use dates with events: 1526 for Panipat, Humayun's restoration, and Akbar's long consolidation period.
