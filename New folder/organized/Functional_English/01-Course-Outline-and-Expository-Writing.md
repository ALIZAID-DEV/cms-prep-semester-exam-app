# Lecture Explanation
Functional English I:
- Ye course students ke basic English language skills develop karne ke liye hai — especially communicative proficiency. Students ko "English for Academic Purposes" par kaam karwaya jayega, jisme tasks jaise Introducing Oneself, Describing Places and Things, Obtaining and Giving Information, aur Recounting Past Events shamil hain. 
- Grammar ka structured coverage "Oxford Practice Grammar (Units 01 to 53)" se diya gaya hai — that provides systematic practice in grammar rules and exercises. 
- Teaching practical focus par hoga: speaking, listening (implicitly through communicative activities), writing short recounts and descriptions, aur accurate use of grammar.

Expository Writing:
- Ye sequential course Functional English ke upar build karta hai taake students clear, concise aur coherent written texts produce kar saken. Course ka core focus hai writing process: pre-writing (brainstorming, free-writing, mind-mapping, listing, questioning, outlining), drafting (three-stage drafting), revising/editing, proofreading, aur peer review.
- Students ko essay organization sikhaya jayega: engaging introduction aur hook, clear thesis statement, well-developed body paragraphs (topic sentences, supporting evidence, transitions), aur effective conclusions. Cohesion aur coherence ki techniques cover hongi.
- Different types of expository writing sikhaye jayenge: Description, Illustration, Classification, Cause and Effect, Process Analysis, Comparative Analysis — har type ka purpose aur structure samjhayenge.
- Writing for specific audiences aur purposes ka emphasis hai — academic tone vs public audience tone; style adjustments; objectivity; persuasive vs informative aims.
- Ethical considerations important hain: originality, proper citation and referencing (APA, MLA ya other styles), integrating quotes (quoting, paraphrasing, summarizing) aur avoiding plagiarism.
- Practical component: suggested ke students ek writing portfolio banayen jisme various expository texts ho aur course-end par present karein. Recommended texts (St. Martin’s Guide to Writing, They Say / I Say, The Elements of Style, etc.) additional reference materials ke liye diye gaye hain.

# Key Exam Points
- Functional English I:
  - Basic communicative tasks: introducing oneself, describing places/things, obtaining/giving information, recounting past events — ability to produce short spoken/written responses.
  - Grammar competency as per Oxford Practice Grammar (Units 1–53) — common grammar items: tenses, modals, conditionals, passive voice, reported speech, articles, prepositions, subject-verb agreement, sentence structure.
  - Use of English for Academic Purposes (basic academic vocabulary and register).

- Expository Writing:
  - Writing process steps: pre-writing techniques (identify and apply brainstorming, mind-mapping), drafting stages, revising/editing checklists, proofreading marks/techniques.
  - Essay structure: craft a hook, write a clear thesis statement, construct topic sentences, provide supporting evidence, use transitional devices, write effective conclusions.
  - Types of expository essays and how to structure each: description, illustration, classification, cause & effect, process analysis, comparative analysis.
  - Audience and purpose: adjust tone, style, formality for academic vs public audiences.
  - Ethical writing: citation styles (APA/MLA basics), avoiding plagiarism, integrating quotes/paraphrases/summaries correctly.
  - Practical assessment: portfolio preparation and presentation (if used) — selection and revision of best pieces.
