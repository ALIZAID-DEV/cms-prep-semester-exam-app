Lecture Explanation
- Definition: Transmission Medium ya Communication Channel woh path hai jo sender aur receiver ke darmiyan data transfer ke liye use hota hai. Data electromagnetic/electrical signals ki form mein wires, atmosphere, ya vacuum se transfer hota hai.
- Characteristics: Teen main characteristics hain — Delivery (data ko correct destination aur correct order mein pohanchana), Accuracy (data bina alteration ke pohanchna), aur Timeliness (data time par pohanche warna useless).
- Guided Media (Wired/Bounded): Signals physical cables ke through jate hain.
  - Twisted Pair Cable: Do insulated copper wires twisted together; interference kam karne ke liye twist hota hai. Do types: UTP (Unshielded Twisted Pair) — cheap, easy to install, thoda vulnerable to interference; STP (Shielded Twisted Pair) — special coating shielding ke liye, used in higher-speed Ethernet/telephone links.
  - Coaxial Cable: Outer plastic covering aur do parallel conductors; commonly used in cable TV aur analog television networks.
  - Fiber-Optic Cable: Data light signals ki form mein; core (glass/plastic) aur cladding jo light ko reflect karne mein help karta hai. High speed aur large data volumes ke liye use hota hai.
- Unguided Media (Wireless/Unbounded): Physical medium nahi hota; electromagnetic waves free space mein travel karte hain.
  - Radio Waves: Omnidirectional, easy to generate, buildings ko penetrate kar sakte hain; use: FM/AM radio, TV, cordless phones.
  - Microwaves: Line-of-sight transmission; antennas ko align karna zaroori hai. Distance ka relation antenna height ke sath hota hai. Types: Terrestrial (earth-based antennas) aur Satellite (ek antenna orbit mein).
  - Infrared: Infrared light use hoti hai (LED transmitters, photodiode receivers). Short-range, line-of-sight, walls penetrate nahi karti; use: wireless LANs, short links.
- Transmission Impairments:
  - Attenuation: Signal energy ka loss medium resistance ki wajah se; distance ke sath badhta hai; measured in decibels (dB). Amplifiers se signal restore kia jata hai.
  - Distortion: Signal ki shape change ho jati hai kyunki composite signal ke components different speeds se travel karte hain aur alag times par arrive karte hain — phase differences create hoti hain.
  - Noise: Unwanted signals jo original data ke sath mix ho jate hain. Types mentioned: induced, crosstalk, thermal, impulse. Noise signal ko damage kar sakta hai.

Key Exam Points
- Transmission Medium = path between sender and receiver; can be guided (wired) or unguided (wireless).
- Delivery, Accuracy, Timeliness are fundamental characteristics; agar timeliness miss ho to data ka value khatam ho sakta hai.
- Guided media examples:
  - Twisted Pair: two insulated copper wires twisted; UTP (cheap, vulnerable), STP (shielded, used for higher speeds).
  - Coaxial: used for cable TV and analog networks.
  - Fiber-Optic: transmits light; core + cladding for reflection; very high bandwidth and long-distance suitability.
- Unguided media examples:
  - Radio: omnidirectional, penetrates buildings.
  - Microwave: line-of-sight, distance related to antenna height, terrestrial vs satellite.
  - Infrared: short-range, line-of-sight, cannot penetrate walls; uses LED + photodiode.
- Impairments:
  - Attenuation measured in decibels; amplifiers or repeaters used to restore signal.
  - Distortion caused by different propagation speeds of signal components → phase differences.
  - Noise types: induced, crosstalk, thermal, impulse — can corrupt signals.
- Practical exam tips: Be able to list examples of each media, contrast UTP vs STP, state which media are line-of-sight vs omnidirectional, and define attenuation/distortion/noise with simple causes and remedies.
