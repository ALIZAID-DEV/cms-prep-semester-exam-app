Lecture Explanation
- Definition of Accounting:
  Accounting kya hai — Accounting is the process of identifying, recording, classifying, summarizing, and reporting financial transactions. Ye process decision-making ke liye useful information provide karta hai. It follows accounting principles and helps measure business performance. (Example jo note mein diya gaya: recording cash sales.)

- What is Financial Statement:
  Financial statements formal reports hain jo business ki financial performance aur position show karte hain. Ye period ke end par prepare kiye jate hain and they help owners and investors. Main statements include Income Statement and Balance Sheet. They follow accounting standards.

- Types of Financial Statements:
  Main statements: Income Statement, Statement of Retained Earnings, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement. Har statement ka different purpose hai — collectively financial data ko summarize kar ke decision-making support karte hain. Ye statements periodic prepare hote hain.

- Managerial vs Financial Accounting:
  Financial accounting reports external users (owners, investors, creditors) ke liye prepare karta hai and it follows standards. Managerial accounting internal use (managers) ke liye flexible information provide karta hai. Dono business decisions mein help karte hain.

- Organization Types:
  Notes mein teen basic types diye gaye: sole proprietorship (one owner), partnership (two or more owners), corporation (separate legal entity). Har type ka legal aur accounting implications alag hote hain — ownership reporting ko affect karti hai.

- Accounting Cycle:
  Accounting cycle transactions se start hoti hai aur financial statements par end hoti hai. Steps noted: journalizing, posting, trial balance, adjustments, statements, closing. Ye cycle har period repeat hoti hai aur accuracy ensure karti hai. Common exam topic hai.

- Users of Financial Accounting:
  Users: owners, investors, creditors, banks, government, employees, management. Har user apne decisions ke liye reports use karta hai (e.g., investors profitability check karte hain, banks creditworthiness assess karte hain, government taxes verify karti hai).

- Accounting Equation:
  Fundamental equation: Assets = Liabilities + Capital. Revenue capital increase karta hai; expenses aur drawings capital reduce karte hain. Retained earnings profits ko accumulate karti hain. Current assets examples: cash, receivables.

- Analysis of Transactions:
  Har transaction kam az kam do accounts ko affect karta hai — accounting equation balanced rehta hai. Analyze karna hota hai ki kaunse accounts increase/decrease hue aur debit/credit kya hain. (Example: cash purchase of equipment.)

- General Journal:
  General journal chronological record hota hai transactions ka — book of original entry. Har entry mein date, accounts, debit, credit, narration hoti hai. Entries phir ledger mein post ki jati hain.

- Ledger:
  Ledger individual accounts contain karta hai. Transactions journal se post hoti hain. Ledger account balances show karta hai aur trial balance prepare karne mein help karta hai — book of final entry.

- Trial Balance:
  Trial balance sab ledger balances list karta hai. Total debits = total credits hona chahiye. Ye arithmetic accuracy check karta hai and adjustments se pehle prepare hota hai. Phir bhi kuch errors exist kar sakti hain.

- Adjusting Entries and Adjusted Trial Balance:
  Adjusting entries revenues aur expenses update karte hain — accruals aur prepayments record karne ke liye period end par kiye jate hain (matching principle ensure karne ke liye). Adjusted trial balance adjustments ke baad prepared hota hai aur financial statements ka basis banta hai.

- Financial Statements (from adjusted trial balance):
  Income Statement (profit/loss), Statement of Retained Earnings (changes in retained earnings), Balance Sheet (assets, liabilities, equity). Ye performance summarize karte hain.

- Closing Entries:
  Closing entries temporary accounts (revenues, expenses, drawings) ko retained earnings/capital mein transfer karte hain. Is se new period zero balances se start hota hai; permanent accounts open rehte hain.

Key Exam Points
- Understand and memorize the Accounting Equation: Assets = Liabilities + Capital.
- Be able to explain and list the major Financial Statements and their purposes (Income Statement, Statement of Retained Earnings, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement).
- Accounting Cycle steps: journalizing → posting → trial balance → adjustments → adjusted trial balance → financial statements → closing entries.
- Prepare and analyze basic General Journal entries and know how postings move to Ledger accounts.
- Purpose and preparation of Trial Balance and Adjusted Trial Balance; why debits must equal credits.
- Nature and purpose of Adjusting Entries (accruals, prepayments) and the Matching Principle.
- Closing Entries: which accounts are closed and why; effect on retained earnings/capital.
- Distinguish Financial Accounting (external, standards) vs Managerial Accounting (internal, flexible).
- Types of business organizations and basic reporting/legal differences (sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation).
- Identify users of financial reports and their typical needs (investor, creditor, bank, government, employees).
